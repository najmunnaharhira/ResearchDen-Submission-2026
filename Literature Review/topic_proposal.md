# Topic proposal

**Topic:** Human-Centered Explainable AI Beyond English: Trust, Reliance and Explanation Quality for Low-Resource and Code-Mixed Language Users, with Bangla and Banglish as a Case

**Research area:** [closest of the nine Research Den areas]

**Why:** Studies of how people understand and rely on AI explanations are almost all done in English. At the same time, Bangla and Banglish NLP work builds datasets and models but rarely asks whether users understand the model's explanations. This review covers both sides and identifies what is missing.

**Questions:**

1. How do current studies measure whether an explanation works for people (trust, understanding, appropriate reliance)?
2. What changes when the user, model or explanation is not in English?
3. What would human-centered XAI for Bangla and Banglish users need?

**Sources:** about 20 papers collected so far (see [papers.md](papers.md)), plus foundational XAI papers to be added.

**Link to my project:** this connects to my technical project, BanglishCheck, an explainable Banglish AI-text detector.
